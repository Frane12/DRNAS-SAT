# CORE 0.1.2-boundary-repair.1

Experimental revision of the existing BOUNDARY100 kernel. The workflow applies the archived BOUNDARY100 patch to its original frozen CORE source, then applies repair.patch. Turn the candidate on with DRNAS_SCHEDULE=boundary-repair. The published default and control schedules keep their original decision rules.

After installation into the checked active set, the candidate selects the shortest newly exported guard violated by the saved repair seed. On the full source CNF it ranks one-literal guard flips by unsatisfied original clauses. For up to four pivots it temporarily freezes the other guard literals, checks conditional propagation with and without the selected guard, and uses the first consistent pivot for one bounded repair. Conditional assignments stay private. Only a complete assignment satisfying the source may be returned. Total extra allowance is the smaller of 120 milliseconds and 12% of the remaining original deadline. The log compares propagation with and without the guard, records source violations before and after, and records whether that attempt delivered a model.

The run compares LOCAL, BOUNDARY and the candidate on Q091 and Q097 plus 100 newly generated instances from the ten BOUNDARY100 families. One logical CPU, a 5-second solve cap, 3 repetitions with balanced arm order, independent model or UNSAT proof checks and separate boundary-prefix audits. A new seed and source and binary hashes are saved before generating fresh inputs. Diagnostic and fresh outcomes are reported separately. PAR2 charges 10 seconds per unresolved attempt. The total end-to-end time also includes prefix audits, terminal checks and harness bookkeeping. The artifact contains inputs, manifests, all attempts, evidence, logs and the paired summary.
