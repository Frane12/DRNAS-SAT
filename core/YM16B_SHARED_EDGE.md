# YM16B — exact two-loop comparison, including angular sectors

Two equal simple cycles of length L share exactly one edge and otherwise have disjoint paths. Fix a spanning tree containing the common edge and all but one private edge per cycle. Physical states are simultaneous-conjugation-invariant functions of two SU(2) holonomies U,V, with product Haar measure. Each private link contributes the single-holonomy Casimir in its cycle (left or right, conjugation equivalent). The shared edge acts by a joint left or right derivative in the two holonomies, with signs/orientations fixed by loop choices; its Casimir is a nonnegative quadratic form. Dropping this term gives a lower comparison H0 = h(U)+h(V), with private electric coefficient e=(L-1)g²/(8a) times the unit S³ Laplacian, and potential -2k cos(theta), k=2/(ag²). The actual operator has compact resolvent by comparison with the positive private Laplacians and bounded potential.

The simultaneous-conjugation singlet space decomposes into paired angular momenta l=l' (integer l>=0). For one holonomy, write SU(2)=S³ and U=cos(theta)I+i sin(theta) n.sigma. Angular harmonics of degree l have radial basis sin(theta)^l C_{j-l}^{l+1}(cos(theta)), j>=l, with electric eigenvalues e*j(j+2). The normalized Gegenbauer recurrence gives squared adjacent magnetic matrix elements k²*(j-l+1)*(j+l+2)/[(j+1)(j+2)]. See NIST DLMF 18.9 and 18.3. The centrifugal term l(l+1)/sin²(theta) is monotonically increasing in l in the radial quadratic form (with the regular Friedrichs boundary condition). Therefore all l>=1 sector ground energies are bounded below by the l=1 ground energy. The second eigenvalue of H0 on the singlet space is bounded below by min(L0+L1,2*A1), where L0,L1 bound the first two radial l=0 eigenvalues and A1 bounds the lowest l=1 eigenvalue. The lowest sector is l=0 by the same centrifugal comparison. No noncentral sector is discarded.

For an upper trial choose a product of identical real normalized central finite-head functions. Each separate Lie derivative has zero expectation by Haar invariance/integration by parts, hence the mixed shared-edge term has zero expectation. Its individual Casimirs restore the full length L on both factors. A finite radial head upper bound U for this full-length single operator thus gives E0(H)<=2U. Consequently gap(H)>=min(L0+L1,2*A1)-2U. This proves a simple ground state if the right side is positive. No assertion that the actual two-loop Hamiltonian is separable.

All three lower cuts use a Schur bound for the infinite tail T>=tau I, tau=e*N*(N+2)-2k, and exact boundary coupling squared. Native determinant sign changes and separate rational LDL counts check the inequalities. Input p op ks-pair-gap LENGTH N A G2 GAMMA. A finite number of couplings, finite graph, no volume or continuum theorem.
