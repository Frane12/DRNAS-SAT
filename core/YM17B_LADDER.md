# YM17B — open SU(2) ladder: two-tree lower comparison

Consider m>=2 squares in a single open strip, with m top and m bottom horizontal links and m+1 vertical rungs. Gauge-fix the bottom rail and all rungs (a spanning tree). The top chord variables are the m fundamental plaquette holonomies, up to conjugation/inversion, with product Haar measure and simultaneous conjugation invariance. For the Hamiltonian H_top consisting of electric energies on top links plus half the full magnetic potential, differentiation of each chord gives exactly one independent SU(2) Laplacian. The remaining physical singlet condition couples only angular representations. Reversing top/bottom gives the same spectrum for H_bottom. Both have the same unique normalized ground vector: the product of identical positive radial functions of the individual plaquette traces, since their relative electric/magnetic coefficients agree and the ground of the one-holonomy elliptic operator is unique and conjugation invariant. The trace of a plaquette is independent of tree frame and orientation. Therefore the two comparison operators have a common rank-one ground projector P.

Write e=g²/(4a), k=2/(ag²), and h=e*(-Delta_S3)-k*Tr(U). Each half Hamiltonian is unitarily equivalent to one half of the sum of m copies of h restricted to the simultaneous-conjugation singlet. If L0,L1,A1 are valid lower bounds for the radial ground, radial first excited, and first angular ground of h, respectively, then each half Hamiltonian is bounded below on P-perp by 1/2 min((m-1)L0+L1,(m-2)L0+2A1). Any nontrivial angular singlet contains at least two nonzero angular momenta, and the centrifugal form orders higher sectors as in YM16B. The full H equals H_top+H_bottom plus nonnegative rung Casimirs. Consequently the same minimum bounds E1(H) by min-max. No assumption that full H is separable.

For the upper bound use a product of identical real normalized central functions of the plaquettes. In the link electric quadratic form, each plaquette contributes four single-holonomy Casimirs. Cross terms at a shared rung integrate to zero: after maximal-tree gauge fixing the chord holonomies have independent Haar measures; central derivatives depend on the corresponding holonomy alone in a common tree frame, with zero angular average. Equivalently conjugation derivatives vanish for central functions, and each remaining first derivative has zero Haar average against that factor. The trial expectation is therefore m times the one-loop Rayleigh expectation at full coefficient f=g²/(2a). A finite radial-head upper bound U gives E0<=mU. The rigorous sufficient gap bound is min((m-1)L0+L1,(m-2)L0+2A1)-mU. Native Sturm sign counts and independent exact rational LDL counts certify the scalar spectral bounds including infinite tails, conditional on the analytic reduction here. Compact resolvent follows from the positive full-link Laplacian on a compact finite product and bounded potential, restricted to the invariant subspace.
