# YM18B — exact energy cancellation on edge-disjoint SU(2) bouquets

This is a deliberately restricted control for the extensive comparison loss in YM17B. It is not the interacting shared-edge ladder. Take any finite number m>=2 of equal simple cycles of length L>=3 sharing a single vertex and no other vertices or edges. Gauge fix the private trees. Gauge-invariant functions become functions on SU(2)^m invariant under simultaneous conjugation with product Haar measure. All edges of a cycle act only on its own holonomy, so on this physical space H is exactly the sum of m copies of h=e*(-Delta_S3)-k Tr(U), e=L*g²/(8a), k=2/(ag²). The one-holonomy elliptic Schrödinger operator has compact resolvent and a unique positive ground function, central by conjugation symmetry (positivity improving heat semigroup on the connected compact group). Denote its energy E0. Its central first excited energy is E1; its lowest angular l=1 energy is A1. Higher l sectors have energy at least A1 by the centrifugal form comparison, as in YM16B.

The product ground vector is a physical singlet and has energy m E0. In a singlet, a nonzero angular momentum must be accompanied by at least one other nonzero angular momentum. A central excitation costs at least E1-E0; a noncentral singlet costs at least 2(A1-E0). Both costs are attained (the l=1 tensor square has a singlet). Therefore for every m>=2 the exact gap is min(E1-E0,2(A1-E0)). The extensive vacuum energy cancels identically BEFORE substituting separate rational bounds. Given an upper bound U>=E0 and lower bounds L1<=E1, LA<=A1, a sufficient bound is min(L1-U,2(LA-U)). No m-dependent error is present. Native Sturm and independent LDL audit certify scalar cuts and the full infinite tail; the analytic decomposition is an explicit mathematical premise, not proof-assistant formalization. Input p op ks-bouquet-gap LENGTH CUTOFF A G2 GAMMA. The certificate is uniform over this restricted finite-graph family at fixed parameters, not over connected shared-edge lattices. The degree of the common vertex grows with m. This does not establish a thermodynamic or continuum theory; the product decomposition is exact precisely because magnetic loops share no links. It is a sanity control for future relative-energy/local-block methods, not a solution of YM17B or the Clay problem.
